About

Made by someone who was tired of messy spreadsheets too.

Radhika Ebooks started as a handful of personal templates that got shared with friends — then colleagues, then their colleagues. Now it's a small, focused shop.

The Short Version

Practical over pretty.

Every template starts as something built for a real problem — a budget that kept falling apart, an invoice sent five minutes before a deadline, a to-do list that never got shorter. If it didn't work in real use, it doesn't get sold.

What Guides The Work

A few rules every product follows

  • 01
    Usable in five minutes.

    If it needs a tutorial video, it needs to be redesigned.

  • 02
    One honest price.

    No subscriptions, no artificial upsells, no disappearing discounts.

  • 03
    Built for real workloads.

    For people managing a job or a business alongside everything else — not full-time productivity hobbyists.
